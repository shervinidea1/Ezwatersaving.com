The EZ Water Saving product

Small product.
Everyday impact.

Designed for easy installation inside a household toilet tank, the product helps reduce unnecessary fresh water use during one of the home's most repeated daily activities.

Inside the toilet tank
Three simple steps

Designed to be easy from the start.

The product works quietly in the background after it is installed. Your normal household routine can stay the same.

01

Review the instructions

Confirm the product is appropriate for your toilet and read the supplied installation guidance before opening the tank.

02

Install it inside the tank

Position the product as directed, keeping the toilet's moving parts, fill valve, flapper, float, and overflow tube unobstructed.

03

Test normal operation

Replace the lid, test several flushes, and confirm that the toilet fills, stops, and flushes normally without leaking.

!
Installation reminder

Keep the toilet working safely.

Always follow the product instructions. Do not allow the product to block or interfere with any internal tank component. If flushing, filling, shutoff, or leak-free operation is affected, remove the product and review the installation before using it again.

Common questions

What families may want to know.

Where is the product installed?

It is designed to be installed inside a household toilet tank according to the product instructions.

Will I need to change my normal routine?

The product is intended to work as part of normal toilet use. Proper installation and normal toilet operation should always be confirmed.

How much money could a household save?

A family of four may save up to about $200 per year. Actual results depend on water use, the toilet, installation, and local water and sewer rates, so savings are not guaranteed.

What is the most important benefit?

Reducing unnecessary use of fresh water. Potential savings on utility bills are an added household benefit.
