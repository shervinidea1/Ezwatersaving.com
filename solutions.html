Water-saving guide

Start with the easiest improvements.

These are general first steps. We can replace this page with your original products, recommendations, and exact content once you provide them.

🚰

Check faucets

Look for dripping faucets and worn components. Repairing small leaks is often one of the simplest improvements.

🚿

Review showers

Efficient showerheads can reduce flow while maintaining a comfortable shower when selected appropriately.

🚽

Watch toilets

Check for continuous running, slow leaks, and aging internal components that can quietly waste water.

🌿

Water landscapes wisely

Adjust watering to weather and plant needs. Avoid unnecessary runoff and irrigating sidewalks or driveways.

🧺

Run full loads

When practical, use dishwashers and washing machines efficiently rather than running many small loads.

📈

Track unusual use

A sudden change in your normal water use can be an early sign of a leak or equipment problem worth investigating.
