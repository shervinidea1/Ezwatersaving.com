The reason that matters most

Protect fresh water first.

Fresh water is essential, limited, and too valuable to waste. Toilets are used repeatedly in every household, so improving water efficiency there can create a meaningful cumulative impact.

Lower utility costs are an important benefit—but conserving fresh water for families, communities, and future generations is the bigger purpose.

Water savings can become money savings

Less water wasted. Less money spent.

For a family of four, reduced toilet-water use may result in savings of up to about $200 per year, depending on the household and local utility costs.

  • Savings build gradually through everyday use
  • No change to normal bathroom habits is intended
  • The environmental benefit continues with every gallon conserved
