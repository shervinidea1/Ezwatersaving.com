Start where water is used most

Simple areas to improve

Focus on the places where small changes are easiest to understand and maintain.

🚿

Bathrooms

Review showerheads, faucets, toilets, and leaks. Efficient fixtures can reduce waste without changing your routine.

🏡

Outdoor use

Water landscapes more intentionally, check irrigation equipment, and avoid watering hard surfaces.

🔎

Leak awareness

Unnoticed leaks can continue for days or weeks. Regular checks help catch waste before it grows.

A practical approach

Improve first. Replace only when it makes sense.

Water efficiency does not have to mean a major renovation. Start with easy habits and maintenance, then consider efficient products when an existing item needs replacement.
